01 🔒 Private Repo Work in Progress
Sol-Fa Studio — AI Music Transcription & SATB Harmony Engine

Proprietary web and mobile music platform engineered for choir directors, music teachers, and self-taught musicians. Features a three-part audio intelligence pipeline:
• Audio → Sol-Fa Transcription: Detects pitches with Spotify Basic Pitch (ONNX), identifies key using Krumhansl-Schmuckler, and outputs sol-fa strings (Do Re Mi Fa Sol La Ti) with duration.
• SATB Harmony Generator: Voice-leading constraint solver generating Alto, Tenor, and Bass harmonies following standard four-part music theory.
• Sol-Fa Scorer: Dynamic Time Warping (DTW) pitch-by-pitch comparator evaluating performance accuracy (0–100) against reference sheets.
